# Bark textures

`palm_bark_detail.jpg` (neutral grey) and `palm_bark_normal.jpg` are made from **Palm Bark** by Charlotte Baglioni, from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/palm_bark), released under CC0 (public domain). Regenerate with `tools/textures/make_bark.py`.

`jungle_bark_colour.jpg` and `jungle_bark_normal.jpg` (the island's tall jungle trees) are **Bark Brown 02** by Rob Tuytel, from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02), released under CC0 (public domain). Regenerate with `tools/textures/make_jungle_bark.py`.
