# Oasis

A VR desert game for Meta Quest 3 (three.js and Vite): a four kilometre desert with an oasis, a floating jungle island to start on, light survival and crafting, and giant creatures to climb. Ark-lite, with Shadow of the Colossus at the heart.

The long, detailed write-up of every feature (how each thing works, the numbers, the oddities) used to live here. It is now in `docs/readme-archive.md`: search it for the feature you need, do not read it whole.

## Explore

Open the published page in Meta Quest Browser and choose **Enter VR**. Left thumbstick walks, right thumbstick turns smoothly, hold the left stick down to move faster. Click both thumbsticks together (T on a keyboard) for turbo, 10 times speed, for crossing the 4 km world. No teleport or snap turning.

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, mouse to look, Shift to move faster, Q/E to turn, Escape opens the controls. Touch screens have a movement pad and drag-to-look.

## Run

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev      # port 4173
npm test         # quick checks, no browser
npm run build
```

WebXR needs HTTPS, or localhost in development. Pushing to `main` builds and deploys to GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`; Settings, Pages, source must be GitHub Actions).

## Where things are

- `src/world.js`, `src/zones.js`, `src/terrain*.js`: the 4 km height field, zones and the terrain tiles. `src/materials.js`: sand, sky and water shaders.
- `src/main.js`: input and rendering. `src/hands.js`: VR hands and held things. `src/survival.js`: health, food, water, stamina.
- Tools and pickups: `src/axe.js`, `spear.js`, `pickaxe.js`, `torch.js`, `backpack.js`, `campfire`. Mining and finds: `src/mining.js`, `src/desert-finds.js`.
- Building (`src/building.js`, `src/building-kit.js`, models `public/models/building/`): start with a small test kit in the watch menu's Pack tab. Choose a foundation and **Place it**, aim at fairly level dry ground and trigger/click to confirm. Floors, walls, doorway/window walls, stairs, pillars and roofs snap to the kit's 3 m grid; floors also join wall tops for another storey. B on the right / X on the left rotates (desktop R; touch Rotate); Y cancels. A green ghost is valid, a red ghost explains why it cannot go there. Placement continues while you have more of that piece. Fit a door into a doorway; point and trigger to open/close it (desktop F, touch a tap while aiming). Craft recipes use wood, stone and fibre. Floors support the player, walls and doors block walking and arrows, and foundations clear grass beneath them. Buildings and door states are included when saving is enabled with `?save=1`. The kit keeps its existing flat material colours; textures can be added later.
- The bow (`src/bow.js`, `src/archery.js`, models `public/models/bow/`): beside the starting tools on the island and at the oasis. Squeeze a grip to hold it in either hand; a quiver appears behind the opposite shoulder. Reach over that shoulder and squeeze the free grip for an arrow. Bring it to the middle of the string to nock it, keep squeezing and pull back, then release that grip to shoot. Pull farther for more speed and damage against the stinger. A tiny pull can be relaxed without firing; grip the nocked arrow again to draw it. Letting go of the bow cancels the draw. Arrows stop at terrain and mining rocks and can be picked up again; release an arrow back at the quiver to put it away. Ammo is unlimited for this test version, with at most 32 loose arrows and a two-minute lifetime. The bow uses the normal hip and chest storage, and the quiver needs no backpack.
- The sand sail kart (`src/sand-kart.js`, model `public/models/sand-kart/`): waits on flat sand by the oasis start. Grab its handle to sit in and roll, turn the handle to steer, let go to coast to a stop. Hands lock onto the grips (either hand, one is enough); seated, your view climbs and rolls with the kart. Desktop: F by the kart, A/D steer.
- The glider (`src/glider.js`, model `public/models/glider/`): you start with one in the inventory. Raise both hands above your head and squeeze both grips to open it; step off the island's edge (or a cliff) and it flies. Lower a hand to bank and turn, hands forward to dive, back to float; let go with both hands to fold it away. Landings do no harm. Desktop: G opens it, A/D bank, W/S pitch.
- The survival watch (`src/watch.js`, model `public/models/watch/`, built by `tools/watch/build_watch.py`): on your left wrist. Turn it toward your eyes and it lights up: the time (sun or moon, and the day's progress) and bars for health, hunger, water and stamina, green to red (a low one blinks). Tap it with your right index finger (or press Y) and the menu grows out of it and hangs in front of you. Check it without a headset: `npm run dev`, then `tools/watch/index.html?view=close&hour=14&water=15` (views: glance, close, away, tap); `python3 tools/watch/render_watch.py outdir` renders the model in Blender.
- The watch menu (`src/survivor-menu.js`): the watch scaled up. Its screen is drawn like the watch's (dark teal, an instrument grid, green for what is chosen), inside the watch's case (`public/models/watch/watch_menu_frame.glb`, built by `tools/watch/build_menu_frame.py`). The watch and the frame reflect a small studio environment (`src/gadget-env.js`) that fades with the daylight, so their metal is not flat black. Pack (what you carry, and what you wear on your hips and back) and Craft (recipes; a gold dot when you have everything), big tiles on the left, a card on the right with what you can do. In VR it hangs at arm's length: poke it with either index finger, or point and pull the trigger. Desktop: Tab/I or the Inventory button. Check it without a headset: `npm run dev`, then `tools/menu-lab/index.html?tab=craft&pick=recipe:axe` (or `?vr=1` for the 3D panel and a poking finger).
- Sky light on the solid models (`src/sky-environment.js`): an environment map painted from the day/night cycle's own sky (zenith, a horizon warmer toward the low sun, the sun's glow, the desert's bounce below; nearly black at night), repainted a few rows a frame as the sun moves. It is opt in: a module calls `skyLight(model)` on what it loads (tools, the kart, the glider, the pack, the campfire, bones, stones, sticks, the scorpion, the hands at half strength). Materials with their own shader code are skipped, so the terrain, water, sky, foliage and glows are untouched. Dev: `?sky-env=0` turns it off to compare, `?sky-env=2` doubles it.
- Sounds in the world (`src/audio.js` `setListener` / `createPositionalClips`): the Colossus's footfalls boom from where its feet land and arrive late from far off, as sound does, and it growls the first time you come within 150 m (again after you have been 320 m away, at most every 90 s): `src/colossus-audio.js`. Wind rushes past while you glide or ride the kart, louder, brighter and higher with speed: `src/wind-audio.js`. The files are made by `tools/audio/make_sounds.py` (the owner's wind and growl recordings levelled and looped; the footfalls synthesised); credits in `public/audio/CREDITS.md`.
- Climbing the Colossus (`src/colossus-climb.js`, VR): squeeze a grip with your hand on one of its glowing crystals and the hand clamps the crystal's end, front on; pull down to climb, hand over hand; you ride along as it walks; let go of everything and you fall. The 1,162 holds are read from the model by `tools/colossus/extract_holds.py` (`public/models/colossus/colossus_01_holds.json`, each crystal in its bone's frame). Check it without a headset: `npm run dev`, then `tools/climb-lab/index.html?view=hand&cam=eye` (views: foot and flank mark every hold on the model; hand and hands put the game's hands on a crystal).
- Handles on things (the kart's grips, the glider's bar, anything new): `src/handle-hold.js` puts the hollow of the closed hand on the handle's axis, fitted by the same contact solver as held tools, with the handle's thickness measured from its mesh. A new handle needs only a marker empty in its model (position on the handle, plus which local axis the handle runs along). Check the fit without a headset in the grip lab: `npm run dev`, then `python3 tools/grip-lab/grip_shot.py outdir` (pictures plus numbers; it flags any hand skin inside the handle or a hand off its axis). Add the model to `MODELS` in `tools/grip-lab/lab.js`.
- Creatures: the dune stinger (`src/dune-stinger.js`, `stinger-*.js`) and Colossus 01 (`src/colossus*.js`).
- The sky island and its jungle: `src/sky-island-*.js`, `src/island-*.js` (every plant and landmark a `.glb` in `public/models/island/` built in headless Blender, loaded by `island-glb.js`, drawn by `island-flora.js`; grass `island-grass*.js`).
- Models are in `public/models/`, textures in `public/textures/`, build tools and labs in `tools/`, notes in `docs/`.
- Saving is off unless the address has `?save=1`; `?fresh=1` starts a new game. Dev-only URL fixtures for screenshots (stripped from production): `?at=x,z&look=x,z,h&eye=metres&pitch=deg`, `?view=...`, `?hour=N` (0 night, 14 day), `?camp=lit|unlit`, `?bird=perch|fly|flare`, `?treelod=0|1|2`, `?start=oasis`.
